## Moodboard

## Palette (flat colors only, never gradients as decoration)
- canvas: #E0F7FA
- ink: #006064
- accent: #FFAB00
- mute: #B0BEC5
- field: #F1F8E9

## Type
- family: Red Hat Display
- weights: 300, 400, 500, 600, 700
- italic: yes
- css: https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Red+Hat+Display:ital,wght@0,300;0,400;0,500;0,600;0,700;1,300;1,400;1,500;1,600;1,700&display=swap
Load this Google Font and use it for headlines, body, and controls. Do not substitute Inter, Roboto, Arial, Geist, Poppins, Montserrat, Plus Jakarta Sans, or Space Grotesk.

## Buttons
- system: Square cut
- radius: 0px
- border width: 2px
- primary: fill accent, text field
- secondary: transparent, ink border, ink text
- tertiary: no fill, no border, mute text, underline

## Text motion
- Drawn rule: The words sit still. A line writes itself underneath.

## Materials and layout
- materials: weathered pine, natural linen, sea‑glass, soft ambient daylight
- layout: Open floor with driftwood racks on one side, a sand‑colored counter opposite, large windows framing the ocean view.
- signature: hand‑carved seashell motifs etched into wooden display panels
